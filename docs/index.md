![Canon R7 EXR Converter](assets/banner.png)

Shot something on the **Canon R7** and need it in **Nuke**? This app turns your
camera clips into image sequences Nuke can use, with the colours set up
correctly for you.

[Download for Windows](https://github.com/VMarci2/r7convert/releases/latest){ .md-button .md-button--primary }

<small>Pick the file ending in **Setup.exe**. It installs in a few seconds, needs
no admin rights, and keeps itself up to date.</small>

## Three steps

1. **Add your clips.** Click *Add clips…* and pick the `.MP4` files from your card.
2. **Choose where to save.** A folder with plenty of free space works best;
   the app tells you how much it needs.
3. **Click Convert.** When it's done, open the frames in Nuke.

That's it. You don't have to change any settings.
